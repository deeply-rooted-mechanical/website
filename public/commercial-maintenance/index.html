Maintenance agreement questions

How often do you visit?

Twice a year (spring and fall) or four times a year (quarterly). Quarterly plans suit buildings that run hard or need tighter filter changes.

What does a maintenance agreement cost?

It is priced by the equipment you have and how often we visit. Start with a Free Equipment Assessment and you will get a written condition report and a maintenance proposal together.

Do you cover more than one building?

Yes. One agreement can cover all of your sites, with a written report for every building after each visit.

Do you service boilers?

Yes. CSD-1 boiler inspection is included for maintenance customers with boilers.

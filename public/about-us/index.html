About Deeply Rooted Mechanical

Why we do what we do

We do our work to honor God, not ourselves. We want to reflect His character through excellent craftsmanship, faithful leadership and the way we treat people.

Every person we work with, employees, customers and partners, is made in the image of God and worthy of dignity, care and respect. In an industry often marked by shortcuts and burnout, we exist to be different: to fix systems, honor people and restore what is broken.

Frequently asked questions

What areas do you serve?

All of Southeast Michigan, including Livingston, Oakland, Genesee and Wayne counties. Our office is in Brighton.

What are your hours?

Our office is open Monday to Friday, 7:00 AM to 4:30 PM. Emergency service is available 24/7: call (810) 360-9664 and press 1.

Do you offer guarantees or warranties?

Yes. All work comes with a satisfaction guarantee, manufacturer warranties on installed equipment and a one-year workmanship guarantee.

Do you do plumbing or electrical work?

No. We are heating and cooling specialists. We coordinate with your plumber and electrician when a project needs them.

What brands do you install?

Commercially, all major brands. For homes, we primarily install AirQuest systems.

How often should my system be serviced?

At least once a year, and twice a year for best results: a spring cooling check and a fall heating check.

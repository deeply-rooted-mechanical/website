About Deeply Rooted Mechanical

A Brighton heating and cooling contractor serving businesses and homes with excellent craftsmanship, faithful leadership and a commitment to treat every person with dignity.

A note from our owner

Construction is in my roots. My father brought me up in the world of construction, and I loved it. I lost him young, and I came into the trades as a fatherless young man trying to start life on my own.

For fifteen years, what I found was a broken industry in need of redemption. I worked for people who belittled their crews, didn't pay their subs, paid employees the bare minimum and built their businesses on the backs of those with the least. Some of them called themselves followers of Christ, but you couldn't see it in how they treated people.

Along the way, God put people in my path who showed me what a business in construction can look like when it honors Him and cares for people. I found hope. In 2024, by His grace, God called me to start Deeply Rooted Mechanical.

When a woman washed His feet with her tears, Jesus said, “her many sins have been forgiven—as her great love has shown. But whoever has been forgiven little loves little” (Luke 7:47). I have been forgiven and redeemed from much, so I want to love much, and this company is how I get to do it: caring for our people and their families, treating every customer and partner with dignity, telling the truth when it costs us, and doing work that lasts. No effort, care or kindness is wasted. God sees all of it.

When I look at what the Lord has built here, I am humbled and grateful. Every crew member, every job and every open door is His provision. And to every customer and partner who has trusted us with your building, your home or your project: thank you. Your trust is a gift we never take for granted.

Matt Pastor, Owner

Why we do what we do

We do our work to honor God, not ourselves. We want to reflect His character through excellent craftsmanship, faithful leadership and the way we treat people.

Every person we work with, employees, customers and partners, is made in the image of God and worthy of dignity, care and respect. In an industry often marked by shortcuts and burnout, we exist to be different: to fix systems, honor people and restore what is broken.

Frequently asked questions

What areas do you serve?

All of Southeast Michigan, including Livingston, Oakland, Genesee and Wayne counties. Our office is in Brighton.

What are your hours?

Our office is open Monday to Friday, 7:00 AM to 4:30 PM. Emergency service is available 24/7: call (810) 360-9664 and press 1.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. We hold Michigan Mechanical Contractor License #7118932 and carry full business insurance.

Do you offer guarantees or warranties?

Yes. All work comes with a satisfaction guarantee, manufacturer warranties on installed equipment and a one-year workmanship guarantee.

Do you do plumbing or electrical work?

No. We are heating and cooling specialists. We coordinate with your plumber and electrician when a project needs them.

What brands do you install?

Commercially, all major brands. For homes, we primarily install AirQuest systems.

How often should my system be serviced?

At least once a year, and twice a year for best results: a spring cooling check and a fall heating check.

On the job

Rooftop unit questions

Should I repair or replace my rooftop unit?

It depends on the unit's age, condition, refrigerant and repair history. Our Free Equipment Assessment gives you a written condition report and a replacement budget so you can decide with real numbers.

Do you handle the crane and curb work?

Yes. We plan the lift, arrange the crane, handle curb adapters, set the unit and start it up. Gas, electrical and roofing ties are coordinated with those trades.

What refrigerant do new units use?

Every new unit we install uses R-454B, the current refrigerant for new equipment.

Do you work with property managers and multi-site owners?

Yes. We can assess every unit across your buildings and give you one plan for repairs and replacements.
